## ⚔️ Core Mechanics

Blood Points – The Currency of Darkness

Every action feeds your power:

· Create a post — 3 points (Share your horror)
· Receive a like — 1 point (Your darkness resonates)
· Comment — 2 points (Engage with the cult)
· Daily login — 1 point (The void remembers you)
· Power upgrades — 50–400 bonus points (Transformative moments)

Your chosen power multiplies everything. An Initiate earns 1x. An Acolyte earns up to 2x. An Overlord? Up to 3.5x. A Kraken user's single post can eclipse an entire week of Initiate activity.

Weekly Caps – Balanced Progression

The darkness is patient, not greedy. Weekly caps ensure fairness:

· Initiate: 2,000 points
· Acolyte: 2,500 points
· Overlord: 3,000 points

Caps reset every Monday. Upgrade points and event bonuses flow freely—only your daily hunger has limits.

## 👑 The Three Tiers of Power

Initiate – Free. Eternal.

The gateway. Three powers: Cat (agile survivor), Owl (watcher of secrets), Spider (master of shadows). All grant 1x multipliers. You can earn every badge. You can comment, like, and join the conversation. But this is only where your journey begins.

Acolyte – $10–50. The Searcher.

Unlock Crow (1.5x), Bat (1.75x), and Wolf (2x). Earn these powers through dedication—or purchase. The Crow demands the Bone Collector badge (12,000 points). The Bat and Wolf offer hybrid paths: earn their badges or pay $25/$50. Your weekly cap rises to 2,500 points. Private rooms open. Priority support arrives.

Overlord – $80–200. The Legend.

The ultimate tier. Scorpion (2.5x), Serpent (3x), Kraken (3.5x). Purchase only—premium exclusive. Your weekly cap surges to 3,000 points. Your name appears in blood red. You gain priority visibility, creator tools, Overlord-only chat rooms, custom profile themes, and the power to purchase badges #18–31 with just 70% of required points.
